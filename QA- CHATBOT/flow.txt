# WHAT ARE EMBEDDINGS?

Embeddings convert text into numbers/vectors.

Example:

Text:

```text
"Artificial Intelligence"
```

becomes:

```text
[0.123, -0.92, 0.44, ...]
```

Why?

Computers understand numbers better than text.

---

# REALTIME EXAMPLE

These sentences become semantically similar:

* “AI is growing”
* “Artificial Intelligence is expanding”

Even if words differ,
vectors become close.

That is semantic understanding.
